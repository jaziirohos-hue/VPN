Xalane VPN Privacy Policy

Effective date: [DATE]

Xalane VPN ("the app") is an Android VPN service. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. In short: there are no accounts, no analytics and no ads, we do not log your browsing, and we do not sell or share your data.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not log or see the websites you visit, your DNS queries, or the content of your traffic.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location or device identifiers.
  • The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software.

How the VPN works

The app uses Android's VpnService and the WireGuard protocol to create an encrypted tunnel from your device to a VPN server. You can connect to Xalane servers, or to your own server by importing a WireGuard configuration file. Traffic to an imported server goes to whoever operates it, not to Xalane.

What Xalane servers store

To connect you, the app creates an encryption key pair on your phone. Only the public half is sent to Xalane. Our servers store:

  • a random device ID and access token, and your device's WireGuard public key;
  • the private tunnel address assigned to your device on each server you use;
  • when your device registered and when it last connected.

We do not store your IP address, the sites you visit, your DNS queries or the content of your traffic. While you are connected, a VPN server necessarily holds your current IP address and data counters in memory to route your traffic; these are not saved to disk or logged.

Tunnel addresses unused for 30 days are removed, and devices inactive for 180 days are deleted. You can delete your data at any time in Settings → Delete my data, or by uninstalling the app and waiting for the automatic deletion.

Information stored on your device

Your imported WireGuard configuration files and your app settings (selected server, favorites, theme, auto-connect and ad-blocking choices) are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you remove a server or uninstall the app. App backup to the cloud is turned off.

Network requests the app makes

  • Public IP check. To show your current IP address on the Home screen, the app sends a request to api.ipify.org, a third-party service. That service necessarily sees your IP address when it answers. The app does not store the result.
  • Server latency. The app may contact the servers in your imported configurations to measure response time.
  • Ad and tracker blocking (optional). If you turn this on, DNS queries made while connected are sent to AdGuard DNS (94.140.14.14, 94.140.15.15), which blocks known ad and tracker domains. AdGuard's own privacy policy applies to that service.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when Xalane-operated servers or subscriptions are added, we will update this page and the effective date above.
