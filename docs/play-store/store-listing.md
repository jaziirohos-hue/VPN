**Full description** (max 4000):
```
Xalane VPN is a fast, simple and private VPN app built on WireGuard, the modern VPN protocol trusted for its speed and strong encryption.

ONE-TAP PROTECTION
Tap the big button to connect. Xalane shows a clear "Connection secure" status, a live session timer, and your download and upload speed.

USE YOUR OWN SERVERS
Import any standard WireGuard configuration file (.conf) from your VPN provider or your own server. Add as many servers as you like, star your favorites, and let "Fastest server" pick the quickest one for you.

SEE YOUR IP
Xalane shows your public IP address before you connect, and the VPN server's address after, so you always know your connection is protected.

PRIVACY FEATURES
• Block ads and trackers with secure DNS while connected
• Auto-connect when the app opens
• Quick access to Android's kill switch ("Block connections without VPN")

NO ACCOUNT, NO TRACKING
No sign-up, no analytics, no ads. Your server configs and settings stay on your phone.

BEAUTIFUL AND EASY
A clean dark design with a turquoise accent, plus a light theme.

Xalane VPN uses Android's VpnService to create an encrypted tunnel to the server you choose.
```
