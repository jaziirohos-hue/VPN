Live prototype

Tap through it like the real app

  • Splash, then connect. The prototype opens on the splash screen and moves to Home. Tap the large button. It shows a short connecting state, then glows blue with a running session timer. Tap again to disconnect.
  • Pick a server. Tap the server card on Home, or the Servers tab. Search, filter by Fastest or Favorites, and star a location. Choosing one returns you to Home.
  • Premium. Tap the Premium chip on Home or the star tab, then pick a plan. Prices are samples.
  • Settings. Toggles switch on and off so you can judge the controls. Nothing is saved.
  • Android patterns. Floating pill navigation, gesture bar, status bar VPN key icon, 48dp touch targets, and system font sizes that scale.

Your review

What to approve or change

New in v4

  • New name: Xalane VPN.
  • Dark theme in turquoise, the colors from your sample. Light theme also available.
  • Round country flags and a world map with a live pin on the connected server.
  • Thin large timer with dimmed hours, as in the reference.
  • Splash screen with a shield and lightning logo.
  • Premium screen with benefits and three plans.
  • Home layout taken from your reference, kept in dark and turquoise.
  • Pill navigation where the active tab expands with its label.

Decisions in this design

  • Logo mark: a lightning bolt in a turquoise tile.
  • Navigation: a floating pill bar with four tabs: Home, Servers, Premium, Settings.
  • Home: server card on top, large session timer, glowing connect button, speeds below.
  • Default protocol: WireGuard, with OpenVPN as an option.
  • Server list: Auto (fastest) on top, then all locations sorted by latency.

Easy to change

  • Accent color, button shape, or a lighter navy.
  • Rounder or sharper card corners.
  • A different turquoise shade, or a navy background instead of dark teal.
  • Adding a sign-in screen or a map.
  • Somali or Arabic (right to left) language support.
